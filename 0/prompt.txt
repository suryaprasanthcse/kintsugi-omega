Root cause: The refactor moved `current = session or PostingSession(batch)` from `reconcile`'s body into the retried closure `book_pending_lines`. When `fx_rate` raises RateLimited (HTTP 429) mid-pass, `retry_with_backoff` re-invokes the closure, which constructs a fresh empty PostingSession; its `pending()` therefore still lists lines already posted in attempt one, so `post_line` posts to books:/lines a second time and the grader's duplicate-count check returns FAIL:DUPLICATE_ENTRY at grader.verdict.
Approach: In `practice_ledger/reconcile.py`, construct the session exactly once per `reconcile` call, before the retried pass is defined/handed to `retry_with_backoff`, restoring the deleted invariant that the ledger outlives a retry. Bind that single session into `book_pending_lines` (closure capture or explicit parameter) and remove the per-attempt `PostingSession(batch)` construction from inside the closure. Keep the refactor's readability gains: keep the `book_pending_lines` name and its docstring, and amend the docstring only if needed so it states that a rate limit retries the pass against the same session (resumption, not re-booking). No change to retry policy, backoff, tool signatures, or grader. Expect the traced sequence to become fx_rate -> books:/lines -> (retry) books:/lines only for genuinely unposted lines, i.e. no duplicate posting.
